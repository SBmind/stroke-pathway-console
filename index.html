Data model

The console reads one JSON array of fast-track activations. Replace the built-in generator with a feed from HIS (or an OMOP CDM query) and every view updates.

One record per 99499 activation. Timestamps are ISO 8601 with offset. Events are the only source of truth for intervals; the console derives activation-to-CT, activation-to-needle and the rest, so upstream systems never compute them. Each event carries the role that recorded it, which is what the team chips and the virtual-meeting audit rely on.
